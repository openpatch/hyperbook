Accessibility and small-screen fixes across the cloud: the account menu has a name on phones, the closed navigation drawer leaves the tab order and Escape closes it, the version line and the history's line counts have enough contrast, wide tables scroll inside their page (the AI usage page fits a phone) and can be scrolled from the keyboard, the print page has a main landmark and a heading, and the landing page's header leaves room for the instance's name. In the editor, the text area is named after its file, the open files work as a tab list from the keyboard (arrow keys, Home, End, Delete closes), unsaved tabs say so to screen readers, the resize handles announce their width, and the preview's "Could not show" and "Notes" badges are buttons.
