Class view fixes after a review. Checklist items and ABC editors count as tasks instead of "Other". ABC editors get their own id: before, every editor of a book shared one saved tune. A remembered section the book no longer has falls back to the whole book. "Other" sits next to the totals and is hidden while nothing is published. The answer view names a single IDE file and shows ticked checklist items and tunes as such. The overview no longer sends a book's pages again every 30 seconds, is never cached, and reads an index it partly does not understand. The CSV keeps formulas and line breaks out of its cells and accepts `worked=0`. The cloud documentation describes what teachers see.
