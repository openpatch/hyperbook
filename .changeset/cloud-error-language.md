Error messages come in the reader's language. The server's errors (a group or username that exists already, the storage limit, a refused zip upload, a wrong student login, …) and the form validation messages were English only; they are now translated like the rest of the platform, following the account's language, the instance default or the browser. Students on a book's login page get them in their browser's language.
