The structogram editor is readable in the dark theme: it is drawn light, with its own dark text, instead of light text on its white buttons and code. Completion in pages offers struktolab's `code` attribute (`pseudocode`, `java`, `python`, `javascript`, `none`) and `id`. The editor's Comment button on a selection, and its other pop-ups, no longer float over an open dialog. Its View menu opens into the editor instead of being cut off at the left.
