Every part of a page is a card of its own, with a border like the books on the home page, instead of one long column of loose sections. A book's page arranges them in a grid: publishing, people and delete take the full width, the forms share rows, and the address line in the heading says whether the book is published. The delete card is tinted as the danger it is. The navigation is slimmer and the pages wider, so the cards have room to breathe.
