The editors know the new `dock` attribute. In the Cloud editor, completion and hover help offer `dock` on `pyide`, `webide`, `p5`, `openscad`, `typst`, `onlineide`, `sqlide`, `excalidraw` and `textinput`. The VS Code extension has a docked variant of each of their snippets: `:pyide-dock`, `:webide-dock`, `:p5-dock`, `:openscad-dock`, `:typst-dock`, `:onlineide-dock`, `:sqlide-dock`, `:excalidraw-dock` and `:textinput-dock`.
