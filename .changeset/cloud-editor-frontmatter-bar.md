The page settings get a calmer place in the editor. The frontmatter bar sits flush with the editor — no rounded corners or gap around it, just its bottom border — and the hint "Type : at the start of a line for elements" above pages is gone. The page settings and book settings popups are wider and structured into groups — General, Navigation, Appearance, Advanced and Protect the page for pages; General, Author & license, Branding, Advanced and Features for the book — in two columns, so related settings sit side by side instead of in one long list. The page settings can also remove a page's jumps: a "None" next to the previous and next page writes `prev: null` or `next: null`, so the page shows no such button.
